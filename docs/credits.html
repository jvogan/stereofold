Artwork and diagram

The opening illustration is Stereofold’s chrome-protein and neon-grid artwork, also used in the repository banner. The interactive molecular view renders the deposited 1CRN coordinates.

The stereo diagram uses an abstract geometric model to show two camera viewpoints and their image order.

Desktop walkthrough

A scripted recording of the shipped inspector, driven by the real local Stereofold MCP server. Each scene change is checked against its rendered receipt. The recording has on-screen explanations and no audio.

What it shows

  1. Open crambin, PDB 1CRN; color residues 10–15 orange and inspect atomic detail.
  2. Replace the molecule with hemoglobin, PDB 4HHB, in the same session; highlight its hemes.
  3. Open the bundled daraxonrasib lesson, PDB 9AX6 assembly 1; advance to the interface chapter and return to the overview.

This recording uses a controlled browser host for the desktop inspector. It demonstrates desktop rendering and MCP operations. Headset pairing, physical device behavior and the surrounding Codex or Claude chat interface require their own checks.

Recorded September 30, 2026. Coordinates are public RCSB PDB entries. Lesson contacts show measured distances; they do not establish chemical bond assignments. See credits and licenses and the source repository.
